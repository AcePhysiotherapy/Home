No Refund – Rescheduling Only Policy

At Physio Enlight, we maintain a strict no refund policy for all physiotherapy services, appointments, and packages. This ensures smooth scheduling and fairness for all clients.

1. No Refund Policy

  • All payments are non-refundable.
  • This applies to single sessions, follow-ups, assessments, and multi-session packages.

2. Rescheduling Policy

A. Rescheduling Allowed (Before 24 Hours)
  • Clients may reschedule their appointment at least 24 hours before the scheduled time.
  • Only one complimentary reschedule is permitted per session.
  • The rescheduled session must be booked within 30 days of the original date.
B. Rescheduling Not Allowed (Within 24 Hours)
  • If the client cancels, reschedules, or misses their appointment within 24 hours:
  • The session will be marked as used.
  • No reschedule or refund will be provided.

3. No-Show Policy

  • If a client does not attend the session and gives no prior notice:
  • The session is forfeited.
  • No reschedule or refund will be given.

4. Packages / Multi-Session Plans

  • All packages are strictly non-refundable.
  • Sessions missed within 24 hours are counted as completed.
  • Packages cannot be transferred to another person without clinic approval.

5. Clinic-Initiated Rescheduling

  • If the clinic must reschedule due to therapist unavailability or emergencies:
  • A free reschedule will be offered at the earliest available slot.
  • No refund applies.
